# tools/geo: province map build

A standalone package that is not part of the npm workspaces. It generates the prototype's province geometry and the scenario province table.

```sh
cd tools/geo
npm install
node build.mjs        # or: npm run build
```

Outputs (all checked in, regenerated byte-for-byte by the script):

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `data/geo/provinces.geojson` | 234 simulated provinces. Properties are `{ id, name, country }`, coordinates are rounded to 3 decimals, and geometry is simplified with shared borders preserved. |
| `data/geo/backdrop.geojson` | Admin-0 context countries clipped to about lon -30..65 and lat 27..75. The simulated countries are excluded. Properties are `{ iso3, name }`. |
| `data/scenarios/2026-01-01/provinces.csv` | One row per province with controller, terrain, coastal, label point, area, population, urbanShare, incomeIndex, capital flag, land neighbours and strait links. |

The script ends with validation: unique ids, one capital per country, symmetric adjacency, population totals within 0.5% of the targets, no isolated province without a strait link, and output size limits. It exits non-zero if any check fails.

## Sources

- [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) `ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces` and `ne_50m_admin_0_countries` (public domain), taken from `nvkelso/natural-earth-vector` on GitHub. They are downloaded into `.cache/` (gitignored) on first run.
- The lower Dnipro trace in `inputs/occupied_zone.geojson` is copied from Natural Earth `ne_10m_rivers_lake_centerlines`.
- Populations (approximate, for Jan 2026), urban shares, income indices and terrain are rough hand estimates in `inputs/province_meta.csv`. They are not authoritative statistics. Raw populations are scaled so each country hits the totals in `inputs/config.json`.

## Inputs (hand-authored)

- `inputs/province_meta.csv`: one row per province with its id, English name, de jure country, and the source features it is built from (`sources`: `iso:`, `adm1:`, `adm0:`, `f:<ADM0>:<field>=<value>`, and `free:`/`occ:` for the split oblasts), plus terrain, raw population, urbanShare, incomeIndex and isCapital. Every admin-1 feature of a simulated country must match exactly one province, unless it is listed in `excludeSources`.
- `inputs/config.json`: simulated countries, excluded source features, population targets (including Ukrainian sub-targets), controller overrides and strait links. It also holds the thresholds for neighbour and coastal detection.
- `inputs/occupied_zone.geojson`: the approximate polygon of the Russian-occupied zone. It is used only to split Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson oblasts into `-OCC` and government-controlled parts.

## Merges and splits

- **POL, DEU, BLR (6 oblasts + Minsk city), LTU, TUR (81)**: Natural Earth admin-1 as-is.
- **FRA**: metropolitan departments are dissolved by the NE `region` field into the 13 regions, Corsica included. Overseas departments are dropped.
- **GBR**: Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland come from `geonunit`. The 9 English regions come from `region`, restricted to `geonunit=England`, because NE reuses names like "Eastern" for Scottish NUTS regions. The two Welsh NUTS regions merge into Wales.
- **USA**: the 4 census regions from `region`. Alaska and Hawaii are in West, and DC is in South.
- **CHN**: 7 groups, listed explicitly by ISO code. NE's "South Central China" is split into Central (Henan, Hubei, Hunan) and South (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan, plus Hong Kong and Macau geometry). Fujian, which has an empty `region`, goes to East. The Paracel Islands are dropped. Taiwan is a separate NE admin-0 and is not included.
- **RUS**: every subject of the Northwestern and Central federal districts is kept, plus Rostov, Krasnodar, Volgograd and Adygea. NE's `region` field does not match the federal districts (for example, Krasnodar is labelled "Volga"), so the remaining subjects are grouped explicitly by ISO code. The groups are North Caucasus + Lower Volga (Astrakhan, Kalmykia and the North Caucasus FD), Volga, Urals, Siberia (2018+ borders) and Far East (with Buryatia and Zabaykalsky). NE swaps the ISO codes of Moscow city and oblast, so those two are matched by `adm1_code`.
- **UKR**: all oblasts plus Kyiv city. Crimea (`UA-43`) and Sevastopol (`UA-40`) sit under RUS in Natural Earth and are reassigned to `country=UKR` with `controller=RUS`. Luhansk is treated as fully occupied. Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson are each split with `occupied_zone.geojson` into an `-OCC` part (controller RUS) and a government-controlled part.

## Adjacency, coast and labels

- Neighbours are found from shared arcs in a full-resolution TopoJSON of all world admin-1 features, before simplification. Non-simulated countries are dissolved to context polygons. A pair counts as neighbours if they share at least 1 km of border.
- A province is `coastal` if at least 15 km of its boundary is shared with no other polygon. Coastlines on large lakes and the Caspian count as coast.
- Strait links are hand-listed in `config.json`: Kerch, Dover, North Channel, Corsica–Provence, and Istanbul–Yalova across the Bosporus/Marmara. The build rejects a strait link that duplicates a land border.
- The label point (`lat`/`lon`) is an interior point of the province's largest part, measured by spherical area. `areaKm2` is the spherical area.

## WARNING: the line of contact is approximate (low confidence)

`inputs/occupied_zone.geojson` is a hand-drawn approximation of the early-2026 front, made for a game prototype. It is **not** authoritative and **must be fact-checked** against a current source (for example ISW/DeepState maps) before anything relies on it.

- In Kherson oblast, the edge follows the Dnipro centreline. Everything on the left (south-east) bank counts as occupied, and the Kinburn spit (Mykolaiv oblast) is not split off.
- In Zaporizhzhia, the edge runs from the Kakhovka reservoir near Kamianka-Dniprovska past Vasylivka and south of Orikhiv to Huliaipole.
- In Donetsk, the edge runs from Velyka Novosilka, west of Pokrovsk/Myrnohrad (occupied), past the Kostiantynivka outskirts and Chasiv Yar (occupied) to Siversk (occupied) and the Luhansk border.
- Kramatorsk, Sloviansk, Druzhkivka and Lyman stay Ukrainian. Kharkiv oblast is entirely Ukrainian-controlled, and Luhansk entirely Russian-controlled.
